Turbo Tests2 runs RSpec work in parallel. It is a separately released fork of `turbo_tests`, with its relationship to the original project explained in the README. Use the documented runner configuration to divide a suite among workers and collect their results.

Read the [upstream README](https://github.com/galtzo-floss/turbo_tests2/blob/main/README.md) for installation and usage, or browse the [project repository](https://github.com/galtzo-floss/turbo_tests2).
