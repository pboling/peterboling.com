Nomono centralizes local dependency wiring for Ruby projects spread across sibling repositories. Its Gemfile helpers discover local gem paths or emit path-based gem declarations directly. Callers can supply version requirements; those constraints go to Bundler instead of being silently replaced with a registry dependency.

Read the [upstream README](https://github.com/kettle-dev/nomono/blob/main/README.md) for installation and usage, or browse the [project repository](https://github.com/kettle-dev/nomono).
