The logos repository organizes artwork used by open source projects and records creator, copyright, and license information. It defines conventions for organization and project directories, SVG logos, avatars, and raster variants. Use the catalog and its attribution information together when adding artwork to project documentation.

Read the [upstream README](https://github.com/galtzo-floss/logos/blob/main/README.md) for installation and usage, or browse the [project repository](https://github.com/galtzo-floss/logos).
