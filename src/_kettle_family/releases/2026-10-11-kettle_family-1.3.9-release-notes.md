Version 1.3.9 adds clean-blockers for locally installed, unpublished versions pinned by family lockfiles, including dependencies from other families. Registry-only update workflows also gain an earlier cleanup step instead of discovering those blockers only afterward.

Read the [upstream release notes](https://github.com/kettle-dev/kettle-family/releases/tag/v1.3.9) for the complete changes and see the [project repository](https://github.com/kettle-dev/kettle-family) for usage and installation.
