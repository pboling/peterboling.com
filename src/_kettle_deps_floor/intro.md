Kettle Deps Floor discovers and raises the RubyGems dependency floors in a gem’s gemspec and its configured template sources. It supports upgrade limits, dry-run reports, checks, and controlled commits, so a floor bump is a reviewable, repeatable change instead of a hand edit.

Browse the [project repository](https://github.com/kettle-dev/kettle-deps-floor) or read the [documentation](https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/kettle-deps-floor/0.1.2).
