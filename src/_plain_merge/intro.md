Plain::Merge is the fallback for text without a richer parser. It divides content into blocks and scores similarity to support conservative merge decisions. It also registers the line-based text parser with TreeHaver, giving callers a common entry point for plain and structured formats.

Read the [upstream README](https://github.com/structuredmerge/structuredmerge-ruby/blob/main/gems/plain-merge/README.md) for installation and usage, or browse the [project repository](https://github.com/structuredmerge/structuredmerge-ruby).
