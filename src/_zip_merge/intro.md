Zip::Merge plans archive updates by member path and byte ranges. It distinguishes members that can be retained from those needing rewriting, rejection, or a nested structured merge. Its TreeHaver integration and Binary::Merge reports expose the decisions a renderer must honor.

Read the [upstream README](https://github.com/structuredmerge/structuredmerge-ruby/blob/main/gems/zip-merge/README.md) for installation and usage, or browse the [project repository](https://github.com/structuredmerge/structuredmerge-ruby).
