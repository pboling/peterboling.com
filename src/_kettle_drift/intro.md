Kettle Drift detects repeated adjacent lines that can indicate template drift or copy-and-paste mistakes. Its Kettle Jem integration limits checks to template-managed files and their baseline. This gives maintainers a focused way to inspect suspicious duplication during template updates.

Read the [upstream README](https://github.com/kettle-dev/kettle-drift/blob/main/README.md) for installation and usage, or browse the [project repository](https://github.com/kettle-dev/kettle-drift).
