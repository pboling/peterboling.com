Version 1.2.1 adds access to DynamoDB batch-write and batch-delete operations and improves secondary-index queries with range keys. Callers can perform these operations through Dynamoid instead of dropping directly to the adapter's underlying service client.

Read the [upstream release notes](https://github.com/Dynamoid/dynamoid/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md) for the complete changes and see the [project repository](https://github.com/dynamoid/dynamoid) for usage and installation.
